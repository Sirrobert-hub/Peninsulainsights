Our Services & Technical Capabilities

We deliver responsive, high-quality, and context-aware consultancy services across nine core development disciplines in Somalia, Somaliland, Kenya, and Sub-Saharan Africa.

Area 01

Research, Assessments & MEAL

Comprehensive qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods research; rigorous evaluations; and robust monitoring systems that help teams act on evidence.

Commission an Evaluation →

Core Capabilities

  • Evaluations & Studies: Baseline, midterm, and endline evaluations applying OECD-DAC criteria and contribution analysis; needs assessments; market, gender, and context analyses.
  • MEAL Frameworks & Theories of Change: Results frameworks, logframes, IPTTs, PIRS, indicator design, and customized MEAL systems.

  • Accountability & Learning: Most Significant Change (MSC) methodologies, outcome harvesting, feedback mechanisms, and evidence synthesis.

How We Work

Every assignment begins with understanding the context, the client's questions, and what success looks like. We agree a clear scope, select appropriate methods, and keep clients engaged from inception to validation.

  1. 1. Listen and Understand

    We consult clients and relevant stakeholders, clarify core needs, and recognize essential local knowledge and context.

  2. 2. Design with Purpose

    We develop a realistic methodology, sampling strategy, workplan, and deliverables that respond directly to the Terms of Reference.

  3. 3. Deliver with Care

    We apply sound methods, rigorous quality checks, ethical research practice, safeguarding, and transparent communication.

  4. 4. Share and Strengthen

    We present findings in accessible formats, facilitate validation workshops, and help teams operationalize recommendations.

  5. 5. Learn and Improve

    We welcome feedback and use it systematically to strengthen the quality and lasting usefulness of our work.
