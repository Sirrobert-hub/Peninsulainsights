Frequently asked questions

Helpful context on how initial consultations and subsequent engagements work.

What happens during the initial consultation?

The first call is a candid 30 to 45-minute discussion. We focus on understanding your core dilemma, time horizons, and organizational context. We will tell you directly if we are the right firm to help.

How quickly can work commence?

Following an initial scoping agreement, our advisory team typically begins active discovery and field interviews within one to two weeks.

Do you work under confidentiality agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We routinely execute mutual NDAs prior to reviewing proprietary financial models, board papers, or sensitive organisational data.

Where can I see examples of your previous work?

You can explore our visual case archive on the Gallery page, which illustrates strategic sessions, field research, and institutional advisory projects.
