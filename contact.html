Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions regarding partnership, field mobilization, and consultancy agreements.

What types of clients do you partner with?

We partner with international non-governmental organizations (INGOs), UN agencies, bilateral and multilateral donor programmes, academic institutions, and federal and state-level government bodies.

How quickly can your field teams mobilize?

With established field networks and enumerator pools across Somalia, Somaliland, and Kenya, we routinely deploy teams for inception, digital tool testing, and fieldwork within 7 to 14 days of contract signing.

What research ethics and safeguarding protocols do you apply?

All our assignments strictly follow international research standards, informed consent protocols, PSEAH safeguarding policies, and gender-sensitive (GESI) approaches, with data secured in encrypted databases.

Where can we inspect evidence of previous field assignments?

You can explore photographic and technical records on our Field Photos & Projects page, showing solar irrigation works, canal headworks, and crop monitoring in Somalia.
