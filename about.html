About Peninsula Insights Consulting

Empowering Change, Driving Success across Somalia and beyond through rigorous research, strategy, climate resilience, and programme quality.

Who We Are

Rooted in evidence, shaped by local realities

Peninsula Insights Consulting is a development-focused consultancy. We partner with non-governmental organizations, United Nations and donor-funded programmes, academic institutions, and public-sector actors to turn evidence, ideas, and field experience into practical solutions that improve programmes, strengthen systems, and sustain change.

Rooted in the realities of Somalia and the wider Sub-Saharan African context, we bring together multidisciplinary expertise in research and evaluation, strategy, programme quality, public health and nutrition, WASH, climate resilience, agriculture, and social development. We listen first, work alongside our clients, and shape every assignment around the people, places, and objectives it is meant to serve.

For us, good consultancy is more than a report. It means asking the right questions, making complex issues understandable, building local capacity, and helping people use evidence to make better decisions—in fragile, drought-affected, and fast-changing settings as much as in stable ones.

Documented Track Record

Selected Works & Field Evaluations

Our multidisciplinary team has delivered evaluations, assessments, and strategic studies for leading international organizations across Somalia, Somaliland, Kenya, and the wider region.

World Vision Somalia July 2026 – Present

Health System Responsiveness Assessment (REACTS-IN Programme)

A mixed-methods assessment of gender-equitable nutrition, health, and SRHR services built on WHO Health System Building Blocks, WHO Health System Responsiveness Framework, and Gender Responsive Assessment Scale (GRAS), involving the Federal Ministry of Health, District Health Management Teams, and community health workers.

Welthungerhilfe & TASCO April – June 2026

Endline Evaluation: Water Supply Systems Sustainability (SOM1054)

Assessed the Water Systems Strengthening approach in Awdal Region, Somaliland, against OECD-DAC criteria and Theory of Change using household surveys, KIIs, FGDs, and infrastructure observation, documenting safe-water gains and women's participation in water governance.

Welthungerhilfe March – June 2026

Midterm Evaluation: Multi-Sector Resilience, WASH & NRM

Evaluation across Hiiraan and Galgaduud (South-Central Somalia) and Sool and Togdheer (Somaliland). Combined household surveys, KIIs, and Most Significant Change stories to assess climate-smart agriculture, value chains, and community resilience.

GREDO & Welthungerhilfe March – May 2025

Endline Evaluation: Nutrition in Emergencies (Baidoa District)

Evaluated emergency nutrition interventions in Baidoa District, Bay Region. Analyzed CMAM performance against SPHERE standards, compared baseline and endline results, and documented major gains in infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices.

Welthungerhilfe Somaliland 2023

Baseline Evaluation: Women's Empowerment & Maternal Nutrition

Designed survey methodology, trained enumerators on data protection and research ethics, supervised field data collection across Maroodi-Jeex and Togdheer, and led the multi-stakeholder validation workshop with the Ministry of Health Development.

Finnish Somalia Network (FSN) Sept – Oct 2022

Endline Evaluation: Hiil Hooyo Maternal Healthcare Project

Rigorous evaluation of maternal healthcare services in Somaliland and Puntland, producing actionable evidence to improve maternal and child health facility outcomes.

AMREF Kenya 2023 – 2024

Community & Facility Malaria Research Assessment

Western Kenya: reviewed a decade of facility malaria records, assessed health worker service quality and client satisfaction, and supported rollout of a surveillance system for essential medicines.

Sanisphere Limited 2019 – 2024

Pharmaceutical Services & Healthcare Monitoring

Longitudinal pharmaceutical service delivery, market analysis, and customer-satisfaction tracking across lead pharmacies in Kenya and Uganda.
