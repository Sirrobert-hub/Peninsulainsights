About Our Consultancy

Advice Built on Evidence, Delivered Plainly

We work with leadership teams, institutions, and growing businesses that value independent perspective, rigorous analysis, and straight answers.

Our Mission

Why Peninsula Insights exists

Modern leaders are inundated with opinions, trends, and data dashboards—yet when it comes time to make consequential choices, clarity remains scarce.

Peninsula Insights was established to cut through the noise. We believe consulting shouldn't be about endless PowerPoint decks or generic industry templates. Instead, we dive into your operational reality, test hypotheses against authentic evidence, and deliver recommendations you can actually enact next week.

Leaders

CEOs, directors, and founders facing high-stakes decisions with lasting repercussions.

Institutions

Schools, healthcare systems, and municipal bodies planning responsible long-term growth.

Enterprises

Mid-market organizations scaling operations and requiring governance structure to match.

Independent

We hold no financial ties to software vendors or specific delivery partners. Our counsel is shaped solely by your interests and the weight of the evidence.

Practical

Theoretical strategy rarely survives first contact with daily operations. Every recommendation we produce comes with specific assignments, timelines, and measurable criteria.

Plain-spoken

We write in clear, direct English. Our reports can be read in a single sitting without decoding consulting buzzwords.

Discreet

We understand the sensitivity of executive deliberations and institutional restructures. What you share with our team remains strictly confidential.

Evidence-first

We separate assumptions from proven facts, ensuring leadership can defend every major decision before their boards and shareholders.

Grounding our counsel in the field

We don't advise from an ivory tower. We spend time on site with your frontline staff, your customers, and your community.

Stakeholder Workshops

Uniting diverse viewpoints into a coherent strategic direction.

Direct Field Research

Collecting empirical data where your business touches the real world.

Institutional Stewardship

Guiding governance and compliance for public and civic bodies.
